In **August 2025** I completed an on-site internship in the **Networking Division of Tunisie Telecom**, Tunisia's leading telecom operator.

- Took part in **on-site interventions**: module replacement and switch recovery.
- Studied the **RLA (Subscriber Line Network)** architecture end to end.
