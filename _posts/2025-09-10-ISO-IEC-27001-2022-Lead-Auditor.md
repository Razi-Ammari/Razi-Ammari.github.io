## Overview
I'm excited to share that I have earned the **ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Lead Auditor** certificate through **Mastermind Assurance** (issued **September 2025**, valid until **September 2028**). 🎓

## Why ISO 27001?
Information security is at the heart of trust and digital transformation. ISO/IEC 27001 is the international standard for **Information Security Management Systems (ISMS)** — and knowing how to *audit* one means understanding security from the governance side, not only the technical side.

## Key Takeaways
- The structure of **ISO/IEC 27001:2022** (clauses 4–10) and the **93 Annex A controls**.
- Risk assessment & treatment, Statement of Applicability (SoA).
- Planning, conducting and reporting an audit following **ISO 19011** principles.
- Gathering audit evidence, writing nonconformities and following up on corrective actions.
