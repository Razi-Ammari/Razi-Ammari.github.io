## Overview
I completed **Fortinet FortiGate 7.6 Operator** in **July 2025**, focused on day-to-day operation of FortiGate firewalls running **FortiOS 7.6**.

## Key Takeaways
- Monitoring traffic, sessions and system health from the FortiGate GUI/CLI.
- Reading and filtering logs; interpreting security events.
- Managing firewall policies, address objects and security profiles.
- Basic troubleshooting and diagnostics.

Hands-on firewall operation proved valuable during my BIAT internship, where I studied enterprise firewall, proxy and WAF architecture.
