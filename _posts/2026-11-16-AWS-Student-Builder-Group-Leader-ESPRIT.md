## Overview
🎉 I'm officially an **AWS Student Builder Group Leader**! I've been selected by **Amazon Web Services** to represent **ESPRIT Monastir, Tunisia**.

This is more than a title: it's an opportunity to take initiative, build a community and create meaningful learning experiences for students.

## My Mission
Building the **AWS student community at ESPRIT** from the ground up, with the support of the ESPRIT academic community.

## What we're building
- ✅ Technical training & knowledge sharing 📚
- ✅ Interactive workshops & practical sessions 🛠️
- ✅ Hands-on **AWS labs** & cloud experiments ☁️
- ✅ Student-led technical projects 💻
- ✅ Innovation, creativity & team collaboration 🚀
- ✅ A strong student community connected to the **global AWS Builder ecosystem** 🌍

The journey starts now. Let's learn, build, innovate and grow together! 🚀💜
