## Overview
🚀 I'm proud to share that the **Microsoft Security Response Center (MSRC)** has officially **acknowledged a security vulnerability** I discovered and responsibly disclosed in **Microsoft Online Services**. 🔐

The issue was **validated and remediated** by Microsoft's engineering team, completing a full, professional **responsible disclosure** process.

## What this demonstrates
- Identifying real-world security weaknesses, particularly in **access control** and **application logic**.
- Writing clear, high-impact vulnerability reports aligned with industry standards.
- Following coordinated disclosure: report → triage → validation → fix → acknowledgement.

> Details of the vulnerability are kept confidential in line with responsible-disclosure practice.

Grateful for the recognition and for the opportunity to help improve the security of systems used by millions.
