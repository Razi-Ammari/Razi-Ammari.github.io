## Overview
Thrilled to share that I've earned the **Oracle Cloud Infrastructure 2025 Certified Networking Professional** certification ✨ (issued **October 2025**, valid until **October 2027**).

It validates the ability to **design, deploy and secure advanced cloud networking architectures** on Oracle Cloud.

## Key Takeaways
- Designing **Virtual Cloud Networks (VCNs)** with advanced routing and subnet strategies.
- Secure **hybrid connectivity** with Site-to-Site VPN, **FastConnect** and **Dynamic Routing Gateways (DRG)**.
- Managing **load balancers, gateways and DNS zones** for performance and high availability.
- **Network segmentation & access control** with NSGs and security lists.
- Scalability, redundancy and **multicloud interoperability** across complex infrastructures.

## Looking Ahead
Each certification brings me closer to my long-term vision: becoming a **Cloud Security Engineer** who builds resilient, secure and future-ready multicloud environments 🌍🔐
