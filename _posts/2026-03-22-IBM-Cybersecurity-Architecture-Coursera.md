## Overview
On **March 22, 2026** I completed **Cybersecurity Architecture**, an online course authorized by **IBM** and offered through **Coursera**.

## Key Takeaways
The course is about designing security into systems from the ground up: core principles such as **defense in depth**, **least privilege** and **zero trust**; **identity and access management**; endpoint, network, application and data security; **detection and response**; and how these pieces fit together into a coherent enterprise security architecture. It connects directly with my **ISO/IEC 27001 Lead Auditor** background and my goal of becoming a **Cloud Security Engineer**.
