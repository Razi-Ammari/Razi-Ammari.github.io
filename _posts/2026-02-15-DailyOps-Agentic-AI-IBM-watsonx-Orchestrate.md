## Overview
**DailyOps** is an AI-powered **operations control agent** built with **IBM watsonx Orchestrate** that automates the start of the workday by generating a **decision-ready daily briefing** from emails, meetings and tasks.

## How it works
The agent orchestrates dedicated tools — **GetEmails**, **GetMeetings** and **GetTasks** — to:
- Filter and **prioritize** incoming work.
- Optimize the day's **schedule**.
- Highlight **critical items, deadlines, blockers** and workload balance.

## Delivery
- A clear daily plan through a public web interface deployed on **Vercel**.
- Advanced **voice interaction (STT / TTS)** and **document-based Q&A** inside IBM Cloud.

## Tech Stack
`IBM watsonx Orchestrate` · `LLaMA-3-90B` · `Agentic AI` · `HTML` · `Vercel` · `IBM Cloud`

DailyOps demonstrates practical **agentic AI orchestration**: transforming raw operational data into structured, actionable insight.
