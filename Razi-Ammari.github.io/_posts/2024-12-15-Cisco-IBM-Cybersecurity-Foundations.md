## Overview
In **December 2024** I built my security foundations with two complementary programs:

- **Introduction to Cybersecurity** — *Cisco Networking Academy*
- **Cybersecurity Fundamentals** — *IBM SkillsBuild*

## Key Takeaways
- The **CIA triad**, threat actors and the modern attack landscape.
- Common attacks: malware, phishing, social engineering, DoS and network attacks.
- **Network security** basics: firewalls, segmentation, IDS/IPS and secure protocols.
- Security operations, incident response and the role of a SOC.
- Cryptography essentials and identity & access management.

These courses set me on the path that led to my SOC work at **BIAT** and my **MSRC** acknowledgement.
